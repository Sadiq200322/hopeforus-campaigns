Rally for Marilynn Mee

For decades, Marilynn Mee has been the powerhouse voice of Milwaukee rock radio. From launching Lazer 103 (WLZR) to her years on WKLH, Marilynn has always been a force of nature.

She founded the MPD K9 Foundation and volunteered for animal rescue causes across the region. Right now she is fighting terminal cancer — and needs our community's backup.

Funds help with out-of-pocket medical treatments, specialized care, and daily living costs so Marilynn can focus on care, not bills.
